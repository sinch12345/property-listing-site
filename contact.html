Quick answers

How does the vibe search work?

Every home is tagged with how it feels to live there, such as sunlit mornings or a quiet street. Pick a vibe and we show matching homes.

How is the monthly cost calculated?

We assume a 20% down payment, a 6.5% interest rate and a 30-year loan. On each home page you can change all three with the sliders.

How do I book a viewing?

Open any home and use the enquiry form at the bottom. We reply within one working day.

How do I list my property?

Use the List a property page. Add details and up to 6 photos, and watch the preview card update as you type.

Is my data stored anywhere?

Saved homes, compare picks and settings stay in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
